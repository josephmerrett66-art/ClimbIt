# Twelve Problems arena

Twelve sparse boulders linked by eleven jump gaps in a 1200 × 3475 world. There are 93 holds in total, with deliberately varied hand and foot support. Round numbered holds are landings; short wide holds allow matching; narrow holds take one limb. The route doubles back through warm-up, high-step, cracked-corner, rest-pocket, compression, hanging-traverse, fragile-crossing, reverse-corner, moving-catch and final-ascent sections. The hanging traverse uses a low intermediate foothold and shorter hand moves; the rest pocket provides two separate boots beneath the entry. Colour distinguishes sections without route lines.

Any physically supported matched-hand stance can charge. Jump order records all eleven required gaps, and completion requires climbing beyond the eleventh landing to match the final hold for one controlled second.

Cracked handholds in the third and seventh problems crumble after five cumulative seconds of occupation. Matching does not double the wear rate, releasing does not reset it, and cracks turn red as failure approaches. Broken surfaces disappear and cannot be caught again. The eighth landing target (entering problem nine) travels horizontally 18 units either side of its origin over six seconds. A visible rail marks the travel; catching it locks it while occupied so the recovery stance remains stable. These are simulation-time effects and pause with the game.

Practice retry restores the last matched landing with a planted foot. It preserves exact body and jump state and labels completion as practice. Ordinary restart begins a fresh uninterrupted run.

The shared load-based fatigue system stays live throughout the circuit. Players need to use planted footholds and matched landing holds to unload or alternate their arms. Practice retry preserves saved stamina, hold wear, broken surfaces and moving-hold state. Restart creates a fresh arena without modifying the authored level template.

Validation: `tests/jump-lab.test.ts` runs all twelve climbs using begin/end limb inputs and fixed physics ticks, searches charge/catch timings for each jump, recovers the catches and reaches the finish continuously. No section teleporting or stamina refills. This proves a route exists; human difficulty and touch comfort still need play feedback. All hold pairs retain at least 40 world units of separation, including the moving hold's full travel. `tests/arena-dynamics.test.ts` checks motion, locking, breakage, restart isolation and practice restoration.

Hand shuffle and Return shuffle finish the arena with opposing 360-unit hand-only traverses. Each has seven handholds at 60-unit spacing; only entry and exit have footholds. Middle grips accept one hand, so players alternate hands and deliberately release trailing boots. Loaded arms drain 50% faster only while both feet are off and a shuffle hold is occupied. Free-hand recovery and planted-foot rest use the shared campaign rates. `tests/arena-shuffle.test.ts` checks both traverses, verifies that central footholds cannot be reached and compares the stamina multiplier with ordinary holds.
